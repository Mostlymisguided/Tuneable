**🎵 Tuneable - Where Music Meets Community**

Tuneable is a revolutionary social music platform that puts power back in the hands of listeners and artists. As a registered Community Interest Company (CIC), we're not driven by profit—we're driven by our mission to create a fairer, more democratic music ecosystem.

**✨ What Makes Tuneable Different**

Unlike traditional streaming platforms, Tuneable is legally committed to community benefit. We're building a platform where:

• 70% of every tip goes directly to artists (committed to 90% at scale)
• Communities democratically curate music together
• Artists receive fair compensation for their work
• Users influence what plays next through tipping
• The platform serves the community, not shareholders

## Investor Pitch Summary (250 words)

**Tuneable: Democratizing Music Curation & Fair Artist Compensation**

Tuneable is addressing two critical problems in the music industry:

1. **Unfair Artist Compensation**: Streaming platforms pay artists $0.003-0.005 per stream, making it nearly impossible for independent artists to earn a living.

2. **Passive Listening Experience**: Current platforms offer isolated, algorithm-driven experiences that don't foster community or direct artist-fan relationships.

**Our Solution**

Tuneable is a social music platform where communities create listening parties and democratically curate music through tipping. This creates:

• **Direct Artist Compensation**: 70% of tips go to artists (90% at scale)—exponentially better than streaming
• **Active Community Engagement**: Real-time synchronized parties with democratic curation
• **Discovery Mechanisms**: Chart influenced by community support, not just plays
• **Multi-Platform Integration**: Works with YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music, and direct uploads

**Business Model**

• 30% platform fee on all tips (reducing to 10% at scale)
• In-app purchases for wallet top-ups (iOS, Android, web)
• Freemium model—free to join and listen, pay to tip and influence

**Market Opportunity**

• Global music streaming market: $38B and growing
• Creator economy: $250B market
• 500M+ monthly listeners seeking better artist support

**Competitive Advantage**

As a registered Community Interest Company (CIC), we're legally committed to community benefit. This isn't just marketing—it's our legal structure. We're building trust that no VC-backed platform can match.

**Traction**

• Active development with iOS and Android apps
• Web platform live at tuneable.stream
• Growing Discord community
• Artist partnerships in development
