# Google Business Profile Setup

Guide to setting up your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) for Tuneable.

## What is Google Business Profile?

Google Business Profile helps your business appear in:
- Google Search results
- Google Maps
- Google Shopping (if applicable)

**Note:** This is different from Google Play Store (for your mobile app). This is for your business/company listing.

---

## Setup Your Business Profile

### 1. Go to Google Business Profile

Visit: https://www.google.com/business/

### 2. Sign In

Sign in with your business Google account (t@tuneable.stream or company account)

### 3. Add Your Business

Click **"Manage now"** or **"Add your business to Google"**

## Business Information

### Basic Details

- **Business Name**: Tuneable or Tuneable CIC
- **Category**: 
  - Primary: Software Company
  - Secondary: Music Streaming Service, Community Organization
- **Location**: 
  - If you have a physical office, add the address
  - If remote/online only, select "I deliver goods and services to my customers"

### Business Description (750 characters max)

Copy this description:

```
Tuneable - Democratic Music Curation Platform

Tuneable is a revolutionary social music platform where communities collectively 
curate and enjoy music together. As a registered Community Interest Company (CIC), 
we're legally committed to using our assets and profits for community benefit, 
not shareholder returns.

Our platform empowers users to create listening parties, tip artists directly, 
and democratically influence what plays next. With 70% of every tip going straight

music economy.

Join music listening parties, discover new artists, support creators you love, 
and be part of a community-driven platform. Available on iOS, Android, and web 
at tuneable.stream.

Music • Social Audio • Artist Support • Community Interest Company
```

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: Tuneable is free to download and use. You can add funds to your wallet to tip artists and influence party queues.

A: We're a Community Interest Company committed to fair artist compensation. Unlike streaming services that pay fractions of pennies per play, we enable direct tipping where 70-90% reaches the artist.

## Verification

### Verify Your Business

Google will need to verify you own the business:

1. **Phone verification**: Receive a code via call or text
2. **Email verification**: Receive code at domain email (t@tuneable.stream)
3. **Video verification**: Record a video of your business
4. **Instant verification**: If you have verified domain in Google Search Console

**Recommended:** Use email verification with your tuneable.stream domain email.

### Why Verify?

- Appear in Maps and Search results
- Respond to reviews
- Post updates
- Add photos and services
- Access insights and analytics

## After Setup: Best Practices

### 1. Keep Information Updated

- Update hours if support availability changes
- Add new photos regularly
- Post updates monthly
- Respond to reviews within 24 hours

### 2. Respond to Reviews

Example responses:

**Positive Review:**
```
Thank you so much! We're thrilled you're enjoying the platform and supporting 
artists directly. Your tips make a real difference to creators. 🎵
```

**Negative Review:**
```
We're sorry to hear about your experience. We'd love to make this right. 
Please reach out to t@tuneable.stream so we can help resolve this issue.
```

### 3. Use Insights

Check your Business Profile insights monthly:
- How people find you (search terms)
- Actions taken (website visits, calls, direction requests)
- Photo views and engagement
- Post performance

### 4. Encourage Reviews

Ask satisfied users to leave reviews:
- In-app prompts (occasional, not annoying)
- Email campaigns to active users
- Social media requests
- Discord community

### 5. Link Everything

Make sure your Google Business Profile links to:
- Website: tuneable.stream
- App Store download pages
- Social media (Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn)
- Discord community

## Important Notes

### Google Business Profile vs. Google Play Store

These are **two different things**:

| Google Business Profile | Google Play Store |
|-------------------------|-------------------|
| Your company/business listing | Your mobile app listing |
| Appears in Maps and Search | Appears in Play Store app |
| For general business visibility | For app downloads |
| Free to set up | $25 one-time developer fee |
| Setup time: 30 minutes | Setup time: Several hours |

**You should do both!**

### When to Set This Up

- **Now**: If you want your business to appear in Google Search/Maps
- **Before launch**: To build presence and collect early reviews
- **After app launch**: To drive additional discovery beyond app stores
